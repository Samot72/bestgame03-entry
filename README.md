# WIGGLE WORM — Garden Feast

A polished canvas-2D worm game set in a garden. You are a cheerful worm — eyes, smile and blush, not a plain snake — wiggling through five garden beds, eating apples to grow longer and greedier.

- Each bed has an apple target: fill it and a golden gate opens somewhere on the grass; wiggle through it to advance. Every new bed is faster and grows more rocks. Clear Bed 5 and you are Garden Champion.
- Golden apples appear briefly for bonus points, and a combo multiplier rewards quick consecutive eats — chain apples fast to multiply every bite.
- Death comes three ways: the fence, a rock, or your own tail. Full loop of title → play → bed-cleared → victory/defeat → restart, with pause (P) and mute (M).
- Controls: Arrow keys or WASD to steer, Space to start, advance and restart.
- HUD shows score, best, worm length, bed, apples vs target and the live combo meter. Particles, floating score text, screen shake on death, and fully synthesized WebAudio sound. No libraries, no network, no external assets.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
